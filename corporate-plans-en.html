SCOPE AND TRANSPARENCY

What is included — and what is not.

Included

  • Documentation of physical network outlets
  • Recording outlet locations based on supplied information
  • Outlet-to-patch-panel-port mapping when identifiable
  • Digital spreadsheets and records
  • Up to 2 in-scope correction rounds

Not included

  • Configuration of switches, routers, firewalls or other active equipment
  • IP, VLAN, DHCP, Wi-Fi or network service configuration
  • Network monitoring, support or administration
  • Connectivity testing, certification or diagnostics
  • On-site visits or physical verification
  • Sale or supply of materials and equipment

Subscription terms

  • Monthly payment in advance
  • Credits expire at the end of the billing cycle
  • Timeline confirmed after required information is received
  • Out-of-scope requests assessed separately
  • Renewal and cancellation set out in the proposal
Documentation limits: records reflect information, labels, photos and identifiers supplied by the client. RD Networks does not claim that a port or outlet has been physically tested when no test or on-site inspection has taken place. Unidentified or conflicting items will be marked for confirmation.

FAQ

Before subscribing

Do you configure switches or routers?

No. This service is limited to physical documentation of outlets, locations and identified ports.

Do you test whether an outlet works?

Connectivity testing and certification are not included. We document the information and identifiers supplied by the client.

What information should I provide?

Available outlet, room, label, patch-panel and port information. Photos and existing spreadsheets are helpful.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Credits are valid only during the contracted monthly cycle.

Do you provide on-site visits?

Not under these plans. Documentation is prepared remotely from client-provided information.

Do you supply materials?

No. RD Networks provides services and does not sell or supply materials or equipment.
