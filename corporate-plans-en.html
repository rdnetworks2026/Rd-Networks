TRANSPARENCY AND SCOPE

What is included — and what is not.

Included

  • Remote communication by email
  • Documentation based on client-provided information
  • Digital files in agreed formats
  • Up to 2 in-scope revision rounds per deliverable
  • Credit classification and estimated timing before work begins

Not included

  • On-site visits, physical surveys or field verification
  • Installation, maintenance or network technical support
  • Purchase, sale or supply of materials and equipment
  • Fiber fusion, splicing or certification
  • Engineering construction designs, calculations or professional engineering responsibility
  • Credentials, system access or work inside the client's network

Subscription terms

  • Monthly payment in advance
  • Credits valid for the current billing cycle and do not roll over
  • Requests handled according to queue and confirmed timeline
  • One request may require multiple credits
  • Additional or urgent work quoted separately
  • Term, renewal and cancellation documented in the proposal/agreement
Documentation accuracy: deliverables reflect information supplied by the client. Without an on-site survey or specific verification, they must not be treated as confirmation of the physical infrastructure. Visual diagrams and layouts are organizational materials and do not replace engineering designs or regulated technical documents.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before subscribing

Can I request any project within my plan?

Requests must relate to the contracted service line. Each request is assessed and assigned credits before approval. Complex projects may require a separate proposal.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Credits are valid only during the contracted monthly cycle and do not accumulate.

When does the delivery timeline start?

After scope approval and receipt of all required information. An estimated timeline is confirmed for each request.

Can I request changes?

Up to two in-scope revision rounds are included per deliverable. Scope changes may require additional credits.

Does RD Networks physically inspect my network?

No. These are remote plans based on information supplied by the client. Site visits and physical verification are not included.

Does RD Networks supply materials?

No. RD Networks provides services and does not sell, resell or supply materials or equipment.
